## Known platform limitation

Manual index generation requires Landlock ABI 6. The pinned Asterinas kernel
does not provide it, so manual indexing is unavailable there and fails closed.
Roots without selected mandoc/manual pages do not request an index. This is an
accepted platform limitation, not a remaining qualification gate; enabling
indexing on Asterinas requires kernel support for the required Landlock ABI.
