# Handoff: XSH and Laputa backlog campaign, 2026-11-12

This campaign covered the XSH and Laputa backlogs, except for performance
and deployment (XSH-1, XSH-3, XSH-14, XSH-19). It also revived xshi to match
`~/d/ish`, upstreamed gluon fixes, and hardened the delegation tooling in
`~/usr`. It stopped on the owner's request after the last running lanes landed.

Since 2026-11-11, `master` gained 45 XSH commits and 32 Laputa commits.
Everything listed below is committed and pushed.

## Where things stand

### xshi against ish (`docs/rfc/xshi-ish-catchup.md`)

ish at `c30a151` is canonical. Every ish ptytest maps to an xshi parity
scenario, a known gap, or an owner deferral
(`tests/fixtures/interactive-parity/COVERAGE.md`). There are 350 scenarios,
all recorded from ish. On a loaded host, run them with `-- --test-threads 4`.
Under heavy load the fdu and signal scenarios time out and pass on rerun.

Landed:
- the refusals ish makes
- whole-pipeline job control and its edge cases
- the fs builtins `cp`, `mv`, `rsync` and `rmrf`, on the internal transfer
  engine (S1 to S4: staging, journal, worker pool, sync, batch retirement)
- fdu as an XSH app on the `tui_*` libraries, with `fs.scan`
- the c30a151 catch-up

The open gaps are listed in `tests/fixtures/interactive-parity/known-gaps.txt`:
- `command` as a builtin
- refusing dynamic targets
- implicit cd before a PATH lookup
- the `0<&-` lexer case
- `z` with quoted history
- the quoting of history-replay `cd`
- non-UTF-8 command substitution in the prompt
- fdu's typeahead hand-off

Most of these need `crates/xshi/src/interactive/app.rs`. Still owner-deferred:
macOS goldens, raw path bytes, and capture-PTY job cases.

## Open work, highest value first

1. **R6 inbox race — closed on resumption.** Laputa `b4bc8a8` reads the
   bounded request and owner metadata through `FsRoot.read_regular`. Its 55
   xinit native tests pass with the rebuilt seed. The replacement regression
   rejects the preceding implementation: it follows the planted symlink and
   accepts its substituted verb. `xinit/docs/SUPERVISION.md` is updated.
2. **LAP-56.** Wake xinit's scanner for a request from a non-root member of
   `xinit-admin`. The group needs a channel it can write to.
3. **`fs.scan` ambient-fs policy — closed on resumption.** The scanner is
   explicitly allowlisted alongside the other ambient filesystem APIs: it
   scans caller-supplied paths after filesystem-effect admission. This does
   not change traversal semantics or claim rooted confinement.
4. **The xshi gaps** listed above, now that no other lane owns `app.rs`.
5. **Queued and never started:** LAP-28, R10, the LAP-33 and XSH-40 guest
   reruns, and the next Lean slice.
6. **dropbear's package proof** can hang after its SSH checks, which blocks
   rebuilding it.
7. **ELF output is not reproducible.** Two fontconfig builds of the same
   recipe produced different `libfontconfig.so`. This belongs with LAP-10.
